ABOUT STR INNOVATION

Engineering that turns requirements into reality.

STR INNOVATION is a CNC job work and tooling service provider based in Hosur, backed by 11 years of experience in precision machining.

We specialize in plastic molding tooling, aluminium die casting dies, press tool dies, machining and assembly fixtures, production-based machining and complex profile machining.

From urgent production requirements to technically demanding components, our approach combines accuracy, reliability, competitive pricing and on-time delivery.
